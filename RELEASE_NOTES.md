## [1.34.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added
- Item tooltips list at most three crafters (online first) and count the rest; Alt-click an item to order it (#389)
- One Crafts tab for the guild's professions and craft orders: the recipes beside the order queue, a recipe opened in place with its materials, crafters and the order; deliver by trade or mail in one click (#389)
- Wishlist: a delivered item shows the game's ready mark instead of an empty box (#391)
- When the site holds your wishlist or the guild's priorities, edit them on the site; the game shows them read-only.
- During a raid from the site, loot uses the site's reserves, priorities and wishlists; your own lists stay as they were.
- The roster from the site can carry the raid's soft reserves, priorities and wishlists.
