## [1.37.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- Raid owners get a prompt shortly before their raid with a Start raid button that invites in party-sized steps, converts to a raid, invites the rest and arranges the groups from the site's plan, one click per step.
- The Start raid button is also on the Now and Web tabs and behind /gos raid start; it skips offline guildmates, waits a minute before asking anyone again, swaps people into full groups and works for raid assistants.
- The website's raids are used only for the guild you are logged into; a character of another guild is told whose raids they are, and a pasted raid of another guild loads with a note.
